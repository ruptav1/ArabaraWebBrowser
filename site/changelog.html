CHANGELOG

0.7.1 2026-11-20 // CURRENT

UPDATES

  • Arabara checks GitHub for a newer version a minute after it starts and then every 12 hours. When one is out, UPDATE and its version appear in red at the right of the toolbar.
  • Click it and confirm: Arabara downloads the new version, checks it against the checksum GitHub records, replaces its own files and restarts. History, sign-ins and settings are kept; if anything goes wrong, the old version is put back.
  • Arabara's source code and every release are on GitHub.

0.7.0 2026-10-12

RINGS

  • New background: rings of ARABARA text turning in 3D around the middle, their letters standing along each ring, mirrored and dimmer on the far side.
  • Four large oval rings sit at the corners, their tips pointing at the middle, turned a little in 3D; only their text moves, growing as it comes near.
  • Behind them, a dark wall of rock seen from below, made of triangles large and small in different shades, keeps coming slowly down towards you.
  • With the music terminal playing, every beat throws the rings forward: their text jumps to six times its speed and eases back within a third of a beat, and the wall slides faster. Every 8 bars one more ring writes itself in, up to six.
  • In view-only mode ([ HIDE UI ], music terminal closed), press anywhere and move the mouse to run the text faster: the faster you move, the faster it runs, picking up and slowing down smoothly.
  • Switching between MARK + SEA, SOUND WAVE and RINGS decodes the page cell by cell, with a scramble of glyphs, instead of fading.

CALLS AND KEEP ALIVE

  • Calls are recognised: a tab in a voice call, such as a Discord voice channel, is never frozen or reloaded, even while everyone is silent. Before, a quiet call in the background could be reloaded after a minute.
  • KEEP ALIVE is light now: the tab still never freezes or reloads, but while you are not looking at it, it is not drawn and holds little memory. On Discord's sign-in page that is 90 to 100 MB instead of 250 to 330 MB.
  • The KEEP ALIVE button says why a tab is kept running: KEPT ALIVE, IN CALL, MIC / CAM or SHARING.

LONG SESSIONS

  • The start page gives back the memory of a background it no longer draws: in a 75-minute run it stayed between 41 and 67 MB, where it used to climb to 210 MB.
  • The music keeps playing: if YouTube Music leaves a track paused on its own, Arabara starts it again. Once its hidden player page is 6 hours old, it is opened afresh at the next track change, so nights of music stay light.
  • The music terminal draws once a second while it is behind other windows.

LOOK AND FEEL

  • Slimmer tab strip and toolbar: narrower tabs and lower buttons and address bar leave more room for the page.
  • The switches at the bottom (YT, VISUALS, LOW RAM, MEMORY) are plain text; a value in red is on.
  • New app icon: the crimson triangle on a charcoal tile, clear on dark and light taskbars.

0.6.0 2026-10-08

LIGHTER

  • Tabs left in the background are frozen after 1 minute instead of 15 (20 seconds instead of 3 minutes when Windows is short of memory): their page is released and loads again when you open the tab. Tabs playing sound, in a call, downloading or set to Keep alive are never frozen.
  • When Arabara has been minimized, or behind other windows with VISUALS:AUTO, for 5 minutes, the tab shown is frozen the same way and loads again as soon as you come back.
  • The music player gives back its hidden YouTube Music page, about as much memory as a YouTube Music tab, a minute after the music stops while the music terminal is closed. Play picks up where it was, after any ad.

AD BLOCKING

  • With uBlock Origin Lite extracted into %LOCALAPPDATA%\Arabara\extensions\ublock-lite, ads and trackers are blocked on every site, Vietnamese ones included (ABPVN list). It is free and not part of the download; see the README in the zip.
  • The Arabara mark at the right of the address box shows it: crimson while ads are blocked on the site shown. Click it to allow ads on that site (it turns grey), and again to block them. The page reloads.
  • On ad-heavy news sites the pages used about a third less CPU and 130 MB less memory, in half as many processes.

MUSIC

  • Two thin music lines frame the page, under the address bar and above the bottom buttons. They shoot out on each beat and fall back between beats, like level meters.
  • Fixed: a track picked in the middle of a playlist went on to the playlist's first track afterwards; it now continues in order.

START PAGE

  • Backgrounds: MARK + SEA and SOUND WAVE. VIDEO LOOP and PICTURE are no longer part of the download.
  • In view-only mode ([ HIDE UI ], music terminal closed) the background takes the mouse: drag the spinning mark to turn it by hand, and on SOUND WAVE move the cursor over the water to push it.
  • The side code columns keep the same size on every background.

0.5.0 2026-10-07

PICTURE

  • New background: a still picture of your choice (right-click the logo and pick a JPEG, PNG, BMP, GIF or TIFF), drawn as ASCII a little smaller than the page with soft edges. Only the file path is saved.
  • The character is cut out of the picture automatically and drawn in front in much finer characters, in tones that keep hair, skin and shading apart even in pastel artwork.
  • It breathes: the chest rises, the head lifts, the knees rock and hanging hair sways after each breath. If its eyes are found, it blinks every few seconds.
  • Light drifts slowly across the background and the picture's sparkles flare now and then.
  • The cut-out takes a few seconds the first time a picture is used, in the background at low priority; it is remembered, so later starts take about a second.

SOUND WAVE

  • The music is listened to properly: kicks, snares and hi-hats are told apart, the tempo is found and held steady, and the beat is followed even when the kick is buried.
  • Drops and bridges are recognised: at a drop each kick throws up pyramid peaks across about half of the wave; in a bridge only a few rise.
  • Peaks jump on the hit and fall back in about a sixth of a second.

0.4.2 2026-10-06

START PAGE

  • The start page is a terminal: a MAIN TERMINAL panel with the striped mark and the ARABARA wordmark, boot lines, and RECENT, SYSTEM and KEYMAP panels.
  • The Arabara mark spins behind it as a 3D ASCII solid, turning about all three axes at even speeds, over a faint crimson 3D ASCII sea.
  • On wide windows the side columns print live code from the animation, alternating between pauses, normal runs and fast bursts.
  • Typewriter boot lines, a spinner for pending status and a blinking address cursor.
  • [ HIDE UI ] decays the interface away from the centre outwards with red and white glitch characters, leaving only the animation; [ SHOW UI ] brings it back the same way.
  • Click the logo to change the background scene, with a fade: MARK + SEA, SOUND WAVE or VIDEO LOOP (PICTURE from 0.5.0). The choice is remembered.
  • The animation only runs while the start page is visible in the active window, and a hidden start page frees its frame buffers.

SOUND WAVE

  • New background: an ASCII wireframe sea seen from above, a diamond lattice drawn as continuous strokes (- _ / \ | x).
  • The water moves with whatever is playing: the bass swells the big wave and the spectrum lifts the water, bass in the middle and treble at the sides, with a quick rise and a soft fall so it jumps on the beat without trembling.
  • Sound comes from a loopback of the default output device, analysed in memory only and never recorded; capture stops when the scene is not shown.

VIDEO LOOP

  • New background: a video of your choice (right-click the logo), muted and looping, drawn as ASCII with shape-aware characters so edges keep their direction. Only the file path is saved.
  • Videos with a still figure over a moving background are drawn as two layers: the figure is cut out automatically, drawn in much finer characters in its own tones, and breathes gently.
  • Bright videos are inverted so the page stays dark.

MUSIC

  • New [ MUSIC ] terminal on the start page for YouTube Music: your saved playlists and their tracks, using the account you already signed in with.
  • An ASCII record that spins at 33 1/3 while music plays.
  • Previous, back 10 s, play/pause, forward 10 s, next, repeat off / all / one, a draggable volume bar and a clickable progress bar. Keys: Space, arrows, Esc.
  • THEME makes a playlist the start page's background music: it plays while a start page is shown and pauses when you leave it.
  • YouTube Music plays the playlist with its own queue, so lengths and order match the website. Repeat off stops after the last track.
  • EJECT stops the music and frees its memory. The terminal opens and closes with the same decay as HIDE UI.

STREAMING

  • Share a single tab to Discord: the tab is streamed without your cursor or other windows and keeps running at full speed while you look elsewhere.
  • Closing the shared tab ends the share.

LOCAL FILES

  • Type a drive or a path such as D:\ to browse folders in a terminal-style view; images, video, PDF and text open in place.

MEMORY AND SPEED

  • Low RAM: tabs idle in the background for 15 minutes are frozen and their page released; under memory pressure this happens after 3 minutes.
  • Tabs playing audio, using the microphone or camera, downloading, streaming or marked KEEP ALIVE are never frozen or throttled.
  • YT:LIGHT asks YouTube for H.264 video, which decodes with less CPU; on by default.

LOOK AND FEEL

  • New logo: the original Arabara triangle, flipped to point up, stretched wide and filled with thin stripes; it replaces the wordmark at the top left.
  • The ARABARA wordmark is set wide and cut by the same stripes.
  • Startup intro: the logo and title decay into ASCII from the centre outwards, then the interface fades in.
  • Custom title bar, a darker charcoal and crimson palette, and the window opens maximized.
